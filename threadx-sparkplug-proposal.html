Shared vision

Trusted edge building blocks — then the hard step above the device

The Eclipse Foundation provides vendor-neutral building blocks for edge and embedded innovation — including Eclipse ThreadX, ZoneX, Eclipse IoT / Sparkplug, and OpenHW (RISC-V) — with strong traction across automotive, medical, and industrial systems.

Industrial and enterprise teams adopting these technologies often struggle with the next step: turning deterministic edge telemetry into scalable cloud data pipelines, MLOps workflows, computer vision, and predictive AI applications.
