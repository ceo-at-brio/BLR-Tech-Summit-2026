Relevant case

Equipment monitoring & fault-tolerant data pipeline

Distributed industrial equipment across multiple sites with unstable connectivity — delayed or duplicate sensor events, frequent manual checks. The same class of problem JOYCLE BOARD will face at scale.

What we built

Resilient data pipeline

Apache Kafka & Flink streaming ingestion designed to deduplicate records and handle delayed or out-of-order events when sites temporarily lost connectivity and reconnected.

Kafka ingest Flink dedup Out-of-order events Reconnect replay
What ops saw

Equipment monitoring dashboard

Operator dashboard tracking live unit status, historical telemetry, and automated anomaly / maintenance alerts to catch issues earlier.

Live status History Anomaly alerts Less manual checks

When the network drops, the log must still be trustworthy

Sites reconnect with delayed bursts. Without deduplication and ordered reconciliation, CO₂ and resource-recovery ledgers fill with duplicates — and operators stop trusting the board.

Outcome. A reliable single view of field equipment without corrupted logs during network drops — helping reduce manual inspection checks and unplanned downtime.
